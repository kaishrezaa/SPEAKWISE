# SpeakWise — 2-Minute Competition Demo Script

**Speaker Persona:** Confident, clear, and focused on real-world impact.  
**Total Target Time:** 2 minutes (120 seconds).  
**Setup Checklist Before You Start:**
- Open browser at **`http://localhost:5173/dashboard`** in full screen.
- Ensure microphone is enabled.
- Audio volume at 80%+ so judges can hear Gnani Timbre synthesize speech clearly.

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] Hook & Problem Statement
*(Screen: SpeakWise Dashboard with clean dark UI, trend charts, and statistics)*

> **"Hello judges. Millions of talented students and professionals across India understand written English and have great ideas, but when it comes to speaking spontaneously in an interview or workplace meeting, they hesitate. They get trapped in mental translation, rely on filler words like *'um'* and *'basically'*, and struggle to sound as capable as they actually are.**
>
> **Traditional apps teach vocabulary and grammar exercises. SpeakWise does something different: it coaches real-life articulation. Our motto is: *Don't just learn English. Learn to express yourself.***

---

### [0:20 – 0:40] Challenge Selection & Live Voice Recording
*(Action: Click "Start Speaking Practice" → Pick Opinion & Critical Thinking category → Click question: "Do you think social media has a positive or negative impact on young people?" → Hit the Record button)*

> **"Let's see it in action. I'll select an opinion question: *'Do you think social media has a positive or negative impact on young people?'***
>
> **I'll record a realistic response, including the exact hesitations real speakers have."**

*(Speak naturally into the microphone for ~15 seconds — notice the live waveform and 30-second timer progress):*
> *"Um, I think social media is like having a lot of problems for young people. Because, you know, they spend basically hours scrolling every day, so they don't focus on real studies. So I feel like government or parents should do something about it."*

*(Action: Click "Stop Recording" → Notice duration ~15s → Click "Analyse My Response")*
